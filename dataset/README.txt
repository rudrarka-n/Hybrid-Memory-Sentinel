Put feature tables (.csv) here to train the structural and family models.

  * CIC-MalMem-2022: download Obfuscated-MalMem2022.csv from
    https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/malmem-2022.html and copy it here.
  * Your own dumps: the "Build table" button in the Train tab turns the dumps
    in CNNDataSet\benign and CNNDataSet\malicious into my_dumps.csv here.

A table needs the 55 CIC-MalMem-2022 feature columns plus "Class" (Benign or
Malware) and "Category" (family-sample-number, e.g. Ransomware-Ako-abc-1).
